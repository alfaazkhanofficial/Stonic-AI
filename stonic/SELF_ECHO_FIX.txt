STONIC Listener Self-Echo Fix
=============================

Problem fixed
-------------
STONIC could hear its own Supertonic speaker output, treat it as microphone
speech, interrupt itself, transcribe its own answer, and create a feedback loop.

Fix
---
The microphone boundary is now deterministic half-duplex while STONIC TTS is
playing:

1. Microphone frames are discarded before WebRTC VAD/whisper.cpp whenever
   STONIC is speaking.
2. The listener stays blocked for 0.85 seconds after playback finishes so
   Windows/PortAudio buffering, room reflections and speaker tails cannot reopen
   VAD.
3. The old correlation-based self-echo guess is no longer relied on.
4. Natural-pause endpointing, whisper.cpp STT, WebRTC VAD and Supertonic TTS are
   otherwise unchanged.

Trade-off
---------
Voice barge-in is intentionally disabled while STONIC is speaking. This is the
reliable way to prevent speaker->microphone feedback without adding a full
acoustic echo canceller. STONIC resumes listening automatically after the short
post-playback guard.

Install
-------
Close STONIC. Extract this ZIP into C:\Stonic and replace the existing file.
No model download and no setup-voice.py rerun are required.
